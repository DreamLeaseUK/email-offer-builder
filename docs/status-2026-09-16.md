## 3. Decisions this session
- **Two roles only** (Matt): salesperson + master admin; **the approver role is parked** — a master admin
  authors *and* self-approves. Rule 3's approved-gate is untouched (an admin flips a template to `approved`).
- **Approved templates are immutable** (Matt): editing forks a new draft version; publish supersedes; old
  versions stay resolvable (campaigns pin their wording version).
- **Suppression list stored in plain text, not hashed** (Matt: "optimise for compliance with the lowest
  friction — the more complicated it is, the more they see risk"). A readable, auditable register with a clear
  lawful basis beats a hashed list nobody can view. Behind Access, admin-gated removal, CSV export, EU-resident.
- **Retention safe by default** — campaigns purged only under an explicit `RETENTION_CAMPAIGN_DAYS`, so the FCA
  record is never deleted by accident. Emma sets the number.
- **Rep contact details are business data, not PII to minimise** (Matt) — kept as-is under the normal staff
  basis; the compliance focus is customer/recipient data only.
