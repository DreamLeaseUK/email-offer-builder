## 7. The demo, and the new direction (end of the day)

The demo did not go well: most salespeople use **Outlook classic**, and pasting the email into classic Outlook (whose
editor is Word) corrupts it. Matt was interviewed the same afternoon and chose, "all as recommended": send from the
tool as the salesperson via Microsoft 365 ("Connect Outlook" once; a second Entra app, delegated `Mail.Send`), automatic
pre-send checks instead of salespeople testing emails, monday.com as the customer record, Mautic for bulk, open tracking
only with consent, certification in four email programs per release. The requirements, decisions, phases and the
detailed Phase 1 plan are **`docs/evolution.md`**. Phase 1 starts in a new session (`docs/PICKUP-PROMPT.md`).
